# Code of Conduct

We welcome respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, professional, and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code, and give people room to learn and correct mistakes.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and accessibility needs.
- Protect private information, customer data, and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly represents this project.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner, @kamikaze1120, through an established private contact channel. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. If you do not have a private channel, ask the owner to arrange one without including details of the incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly, respect confidentiality as far as reasonably possible, and consider the context and impact. Responses may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or removal from project participation. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited. A participant may request review of a decision through the same private channel.
